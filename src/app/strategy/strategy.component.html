What distinguishes a zero-pollution strategy from a technical solution?

A zero-pollution strategy:​

  • Considers feasibility, effectiveness and possible barriers to implementation of technical solutions, which can span social, environmental, human health, financial, technical and regulatory requirements​.
  • Can be very specific for a circular economy route and local context​.
  • Requires the involvement of stakeholders from the entire life cycle of chemicals.

By combining the solutions offered in the Solutions Module with the information provided below, decision-makers can work towards a comprehensive zero-pollution strategy.
